01 — OVERVIEW

What it is

Four capabilities that turn a quiet listings site into a tireless sales consultant.

🧠

One brain, many faces

A single AI concierge answers across web, Telegram and live voice. A new channel is a new face — not a new system to rebuild.

🏠

Recommendations from real data

Semantic search over the live portfolio returns genuine matches as rich visual cards. Layla never invents a listing, a price or availability.

📊

Hybrid lead scoring

Every conversation is scored 0–100 by deterministic rules plus an LLM read of intent — with a full, auditable history behind each number.

🤝

Booking & human handoff

Real viewing slots get booked in seconds; the moment a lead turns hot, its full context is packaged and pushed to a human agent.

The headline

One conversation, every channel. Web chat, Telegram and a live voice call share the same brain, the same memory and the same live data — so the client never repeats themselves, and no lead is ever lost.

02 — ARCHITECTURE

How it works

From a stranger's first message to a booked viewing — every step, in plain language.

01

A message arrives — from anywhere

Whether the client types in the website chat, messages the Telegram bot or speaks on a voice call, every message is normalised into one standard format and matched to the right person.

💡 The same visitor is recognised across channels — a chat today and a call tomorrow are one continuous conversation.

02

The orchestrator understands & decides

A single AI brain reads the intent, remembers the conversation so far, and chooses the right specialist tool — search the catalogue, answer a policy question, check availability or escalate.

💡 Layla must call a tool before naming any property — she is never allowed to answer inventory questions from memory.

03

Real matches are retrieved

The query is turned into a vector and compared against the live catalogue and the agency knowledge base, returning the closest residences and the most relevant facts — ranked by genuine fit.

04

Facts are captured & the lead is scored

After every turn the system quietly extracts the facts that surfaced — budget, need, timeline — saves them to the CRM, and recomputes the lead's score. No forms, no manual data entry.

Booking — or a handoff to a human

When the client is ready, Layla offers real available slots and books the viewing. When a lead is hot or asks for a person, a human agent is alerted instantly with a full summary of the conversation.

Nurture keeps quiet leads warm

03 — DATA

What gets stored

Two core tables hold the heart of the system: who the lead is, and what's for sale. Field names are technical; what they mean is in plain words.

👤

leads · the CRM record

iduuidUnique identifier for each prospective client.
full_namestringThe client's name, learned naturally in conversation.
business_unitenumWhether they want to rent or to buy.
statusenumStage in the journey: new → engaged → qualified → hot → booked.
scoreintCurrent lead quality, 0–100, updated every turn.
budget · need · timelinemixedThe BANT facts captured automatically as they talk.
🏠

products · the live catalogue

skustringListing code, e.g. RNT-MAR-001 — the card's stable key.
namestringThe residence's title, as shown on its card.
categorystringApartment, villa, townhouse, penthouse or studio.
pricenumericAnnual rent or total sale price, in AED.
attributesjsonbBedrooms, area, view, floor, amenities — flexible detail.
embeddingvectorThe mathematical "meaning" used for semantic search.

05 — LAUNCH

What it takes to go live

Five moves from a clean environment to a working concierge. Enough to scope the effort — no need to build it yourself.

Provision the database

Run the Supabase migrations and seed the catalogue and knowledge base, so the AI has real residences and real policies to work from.

Configure the AI workflows

Import the n8n automations and connect the credentials — OpenAI for the brain, Telegram for messaging, ElevenLabs for voice.

Deploy the site

Build the Next.js app behind the domain. Every secret stays server-side; the browser never sees a key.

Wire up the channels

Point the web widget, the Telegram bot and the voice agent at the orchestrator, so all three share one brain.

Switch on alerts & nurture

Verify the email sending domain and schedule the property-alert and nurture jobs, so the system keeps working between visits.
